# Hostile Workplace

Turn co-op into co-op-with-knives. Enable the **Artifact of Mutiny** and the run plays normally — *until* an **Open Season** window opens (by default when the teleporter starts charging). For its duration, **friendly fire is on** and **killing a teammate steals a cut of their items**. Then truce returns and you're allies again, until the next window.

- **Telegraphed** — a countdown warning and an on-screen Open Season flag, so nobody's betrayed by surprise.
- **Friendly fire, scaled** — PvP damage is turned down (~20%) so duels last longer than a frame, with one-shot protection so you can't be deleted from full health. Burst classes still hit hard.
- **Item theft** — a PvP kill permanently transfers a *percentage of each tier* of the victim's items to the killer; rarer items are stolen less often. The richest player is the juiciest target and has the most to lose.
- **Best with a revive mod** — kills become a recurring scramble over the same loot instead of knocking someone out of the run.

Opt-in and lobby-voted (it's an artifact), so it only happens when everyone agrees to the bloodbath.
